The Logo Call

Six directions to react to — not final art. Every mark is built to survive as a tiny avatar, the real test for a podcast. The question isn't "do you like it" — it's "does it work at 48 pixels, on a tee, and on a thumbnail you'd click."

Early lean (pending sign-off): Concept 01 · The Redaction — one-color capable, instantly readable, on-brand for "truth that got struck out." Second choice for merch/stamp use: Concept 02 · The Versus Badge.

Concept 04 · The Broadcast

A CRT set tuned to "GI." The channel range (1946–2012) = the span of generations at the table. Warm nostalgia that still says "we broadcast uncomfortable truths." Plays to Lucid's cinematic side.

Concept 05 · The Spectrum

A mic under an arc of four dots — one per generation, teal to black. Cleanest and most "premium podcast." Scales down beautifully; the arc doubles as a waveform motif for animation.

Heavy stacked type like a protest placard — loud, modern, unapologetic. The abbreviated "GEN / INCORRECT" is a natural social shorthand and hashtag (#GenIncorrect). Cheapest to animate.
