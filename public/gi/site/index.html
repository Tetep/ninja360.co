The Format

One question. Six segments. No moderator rescue.

Every episode runs the same spine — built so the fight is real but the landing is honest. 45 to 70 minutes, video-first.

01
Cold Open · 0:30–1:00
The sharpest 20 seconds of disagreement, cut in first. Hook before logo.
02
The Opener · 4–6 min
The signature question. Sets every position before the topic lands.
03
The Topic · 25–40 min
One pillar question, four to six sub-questions. Steered for contrast, not chaos.
04
Steel-Man · 5–8 min
Each generation argues the opposing side's best point. The change-your-mind engine.
05
Common Ground · 3–5 min
“Where did we actually agree?” The mission delivered, on camera.
06
Outro · 0:30
Next-topic tease and the Question of the Week ask.
What's one belief your generation holds strongly that the others misunderstand? The Opener — asked every episode

The Four Seats

Gen Z
approx. 18–27
Grew up online. Fluent in everything new — allergic to “because that's how it's done.”
Millennial
approx. 28–43
Raised analog, lives digital. Caught between every promise and every punchline.
Gen X
approx. 44–59
The latchkey skeptics. Saw both worlds — trusts neither.
Boomer
approx. 60+
Built the rules everyone else is arguing about. Not done defending them.
