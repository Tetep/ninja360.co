The Pitch

Four generations, one table, one question nobody answers honestly in public.

We're not looking for an expert. We're looking for someone who'll say what their generation actually thinks — and defend it.

A 90-minute taping. Cinematic setup. You'll leave with a month of content.

Nobody gets to just talk — you'll also have to argue the other generation's best point. That's the part people love.

The Format

How an Episode Runs

0:30–1:00Cold open. The sharpest twenty seconds of disagreement, cut in first.
4–6 minThe Opener. Same question every episode: what's one belief your generation holds that the others misunderstand?
25–40 minThe Topic. One charged question, four to six follow-ups. No moderator rescue.
5–8 minSteel-man round. Each generation argues the opposing side's best point.
3–5 minCommon Ground. Where did we actually agree?
0:30Outro. Next topic, and how to get in the room.
