About the Show

Built to disagree.
Filmed to find common ground.

Everyone's yelling past each other by generation — at work, at the dinner table, in every comment section. We put the four generations at one table on purpose, hand them the question everyone's dodging, and stay until somebody's mind moves.

The Mission

Contrast the bias. Keep the receipts. Land it honest.

Every episode is a controlled burn: one charged question, four generational takes, and a format that forces each seat to argue the other side before anyone gets to close. The win condition isn't a dunk — it's a concession, a changed mind, or a piece of common ground said out loud, on camera.

That rule is also the editorial guardrail. The show runs hot, but it lands somewhere a sponsor can stand next to — the heat is the hook, the common ground is the brand.

It's the lane nobody owns: the production value of a flagship interview show, the structured disagreement of a social experiment, and the energy of a late-night panel — organized around age as the axis of conflict. Provocative but principled. We never punch down, and we never pretend there's one correct answer.

Because the truth isn't always age-appropriate. Tagline № 2
