Tech

Episode 001 · AI

Our first topic. Four generations at one table, arguing about AI. These ten are the shortlist. About five make the cut on the night, with a couple held as backups.

  1. Is it ever okay to let AI write something you sign your name to?
  2. Would you trust AI to cook or bake for you?
  3. Should AI-generated art be allowed to compete against human artists?
  4. How much can we trust the AI summary at the top of every search?
  5. Is using AI for homework any different from paying someone to write it?
  6. Should AI help police or the military make life-and-death decisions?
  7. Should AI decide who gets a job interview, a loan, or a medical treatment?
  8. Would you let your kids ride in a driverless car?
  9. Should AI be allowed to generate images of real people without their permission?
  10. Should AI replace a therapist or doctor for people who can't afford one?
