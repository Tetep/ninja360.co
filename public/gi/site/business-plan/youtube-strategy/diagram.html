GOAL 1 · ENGAGEMENT
Polls that start arguments

Two-option Community polls, reposted as Instagram Stories, X polls, and TikTok question stickers. Target: 3% of subscribers voting per poll, comment ratio climbing each cycle.

GOAL 2 · MEMBERSHIP
Members vote first

Unlock memberships at 1,000 subs. Members then see each poll 24 hours early and pick the next cycle's question. Each show ends on a members-only "my real answer" segment. Target: first 10 members by Mar 28.

GOAL 3 · COMMUNITY GROWTH
Shorts bring the strangers

Result-reveal Shorts cross-posted to Reels and TikTok with one CTA: "Vote on the next one — link in bio." Best comment of the cycle gets read on air and credited. Target: +100 subs per 2-week cycle.

01 — The 2-week cycle · two polls, two Shorts, one show
WEEK A · MON
Poll 1 goes live

Two options only, no hedging. Pin a comment asking "why?" Mirror to IG Story, X, TikTok. Screenshot the split at 48h.

WEEK A · WED
Short: reveal 1

"72% of you said no. Here's the comment that almost changed my mind." Under 45s. Cross-post to Reels and TikTok.

WEEK B · MON + WED
Poll 2 + Short 2

Same rhythm, higher stakes. Short 2 ends with "both answers get argued Friday."

WEEK B · FRI · THE SHOW
Argue both polls

10–15 min long-form. Open on the numbers, read the best comments, close by asking the next cycle's first question aloud. Members-only "my real answer" outro.

WEEK B · SUN · LOOP
Tease next cycle

Community post: "Monday's question is harder." Re-pin this cycle's results as proof people showed up.

03 — Poll rules
  • Two options. A third option kills the fight.
  • Never add "it depends." That's the comment section's job.
  • Polls post Monday 7am local; shows publish Friday 5pm.
  • Reply to the first 20 comments within the hour.
  • Never reveal your own answer until the Friday video.
04 — What to measure
  • Engagement: % of subs voting (target 3%); comment-to-vote ratio (≥15%).
  • Growth: subs gained per cycle (target +100); Reels/TikTok → YouTube click-through.
  • Watch time: hours per show (target 60); on pace for 4,000/yr.
  • Membership: date unlocked; members by Mar 28 (target 10).
  • Cycle-over-cycle vote count; the ladder should climb.
05 — Finale · Fri Mar 12
"You voted. Here's the line you drew."

Show 5 doubles as the finale: rank all ten results from most-trusted to least-trusted AI, then re-run poll 01 and poll 10 to see if ten weeks changed anyone's mind. Announce memberships live.
