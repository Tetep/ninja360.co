01 · The Goal

When a Kansas City business owner wants to get found online, the first name they think of is Tim Petet. Not "a marketing agency" — a name. That's the whole target. Every piece of work on this page exists to get us there.

How we keep score: a 60-point scorecard across five areas — PitchPublishProductProfilePartnership

02 · The Money Rule — Two Buckets, Never Mixed

Every dollar in this plan lands in one of two buckets, and the buckets never mix. Get this rule and everything else on the page makes sense.

Bucket A · Tim's Existing Clients"Ledger A"
The clients Tim already has. That money is Tim's — helpers get a flat day rate, nothing more.
What it is
LiveSmart, Kobler, 54th Street Grill Chain (32 units), and Immersive Data Solutions (IDS + its retail subcontracts). Tim closed all of these before the crew existed.
Whose money
Tim's alone. It funds Tim and the business. Partners never share in it.
If crew helps
When Gabe or Pavan work one of these shoots, they're paid a flat field rate for that day — set in Partner Pay. That's the whole transaction.
Bucket B · What the Crew Builds Together"Ledger B"
Everything new — new clients, the show, the courses. The only money the crew shares. Starts at $0.
What it is
The new machine Tim, Gabe, and Pavan build from here: fresh clients the engine brings in, the podcast, the courses.
Whose money
Shared — by the specific rates and splits listed under each leg below. No new revenue → no payout. It starts at $0 and only grows if the work lands.
Who owns it
The LLC owns everything that gets built (pipelines, presets, courses, footage). Partners earn from what they build — they don't own the company.
Still open: the actual rates, per-episode fees, and percentages. Each one is flagged to lock below and listed again in section 06 — they get set in the room, in writing, before work starts.

05 · The Weekly Loop — How It's Run

One simple rhythm, every week: set it Monday, track it all week, close it Friday.

WhenWhat happens
Monday · Dojo30 minutes. Set the week's one objective. Fill the Mission Board: each card = owner · deliverable · due date. Pull each card from the leg it serves.
All week · ThreadUpdates posted to the thread. No meetings. The board is the source of truth.
Friday · WrapEveryone posts their wins. Count the stars. What didn't ship rolls to Monday with a reason.
The Floor is the only surface you touch. Set the board Monday, post to the thread all week, count stars Friday — the rest of the dojo holds itself. You run the rhythm; the rhythm runs the plan.
