On the Road —
GHL Funnel Build Spec

Source of truth: the living Claude Doc here — this page is a dated snapshot.

Status and the funnel

Tim owns this build, with no handoff. It's five GHL steps you can do in one sitting, then one phone test, then the QR cards get printed. The funnel runs from a street QR into GHL, which keeps the venue and the fan, and then to Erik's Google Form for casting. Follow-up is manual (your phone) until SMS and email sending are live.

The venue slug rides on the QR link, so every scan, applicant and sponsor report can be traced back to the night that produced it.

What exists and what this adds

This build adds one form, one pipeline, three workflows and one redirect. It leaves everything already in the GI sub-account (whkhRNZwHhdpUToE7SpN) alone. Every new object is named "GI OTR … V01" so it sorts together and can't be mistaken for the casting objects.

Casting itself stays in Erik's Google Form and its Topic Matrix. GHL's job is to catch the person on the street, remember where they came from, and get them to that form.

Step 1: Fields and tags

Build these first. The form and workflows pick them from dropdowns, so they have to exist before you build those. That's the same order the Aug 30 build used.

Custom field: Settings → Custom Fields → folder "GI Show" → Add Field

  1. Type: Single Line. Name: GI Source Venue. Object: Contact.
  2. Save. Write down the merge key GHL gives it (expect {{contact.gi_source_venue}}).
  3. Reuse the existing GI Generation field as-is. Don't make an On the Road copy, so street signups and the old signups use the same generation values.

Tags: Settings → Tags → Add Tag

(lowercase, exactly as written)

TagApplied byMeans
source: on the roadIntake workflowScanned a street QR
gi: fan listIntake workflowOn the fan list. Stays on even if they never apply
otr: no srcIntake workflowCame in without a venue. A broken QR or a forwarded link
otr: invitedInvite workflow, or you by handGot the apply link
otr: appliedApplied workflowShows up in Erik's Topic Matrix

Venue slug rule: lowercase, hyphens, no dates. Example: smithville-rise-shine (Smithville Rise and Shine is already on the marketing list). The night comes from the contact's Date Added, so one printed card per venue works every night you go back.

Step 2: Street form and QR links

The form takes 20 seconds on a phone in a restaurant line: five things to fill in, plus one hidden field that records the venue. Anything longer belongs in Erik's application, not here.

Sites → Forms → Builder → New form → name it GI OTR Street Signup V01

3EmailYesFor the apply link once email sending is live
4GI GenerationYesThe existing field and its 7 options
5Age ConfirmationYesSame checkbox as the old form: "I confirm I am 18 years of age or older."
6SMS consent boxesNoSame wording as the old form. Consent you have to check to submit isn't real consent

Then:

  1. Styles: paper #D9CFBA background, ink #181715 labels (Advanced tab → Label Color), rust #963719 button with #F5EFE2 text. Same placement as the Aug 30 restyle.
  2. Submit button last. Nothing below it.
  3. On submit, show this message: "You're on the list. Check your texts tonight. We'll send you a link to grab a seat on the show."
  4. Save. Copy the form's share link: https://links.ninja360.net/widget/form/<FORM_ID>.

The link people scan: Cloudflare, generationallyincorrect.net zone

Print a link you control, not the GHL form link. If the form ever changes, you repoint one rule and every card already printed still works.

  1. DNS: add a proxied record for road. The orange-cloud placeholder A record 192.0.2.1 is fine, because the rule answers before it's ever reached.
  2. Rules → Redirect Rules → Create: hostname equals road.generationallyincorrect.net → static URL https://links.ninja360.net/widget/form/<FORM_ID>, status 302, Preserve query string ON.
  3. QR per venue = https://road.generationallyincorrect.net/?src=<venue-slug>.
  4. Keep 302 (not 301) so phones don't cache the old target if you repoint later.

A separate pipeline keeps street scans out of the casting pipeline. It also lets you report by venue: each card's Source is set to the venue slug.

Step 4: Workflows

Three small workflows. They're built to run now, with no SMS and no contact-facing email. The email step slots in later without a rebuild. Same GHL rules as August: pick tags and values from dropdowns, put any if/else last, Publish then Save, and two saves when you edit a published workflow.

A. GI OTR Intake V01.

Trigger: Form Submitted, filter Form is GI OTR Street Signup V01.

  1. Add Tag: source: on the road, gi: fan list.
  2. Create Opportunity: pipeline GI OTR Fans V01, stage Scanned, name = Contact Full Name, source = {{contact.gi_source_venue}}, duplicates off.
  3. Internal Notification (email to Tim): subject OTR scan: {{contact.first_name}}, {{contact.gi_generation}} @ {{contact.gi_source_venue}}.
  4. Create Task, assigned to Tim, due in 12 hours: "Text {{contact.first_name}} the apply link, then move the card to Invited".
  5. If/else (last): GI Source Venue is empty → Add Tag otr: no src. Otherwise, end.

B. GI OTR Invite V01.

Trigger: Pipeline Stage Changed, pipeline GI OTR Fans V01, stage Invited.

  1. Add Tag: otr: invited.
  2. Wait 5 days.
  3. If/else: has tag otr: applied → end. Otherwise → Create Task for Tim: "Nudge {{contact.first_name}} once about applying".
  4. Wait 9 days.
  5. If/else: has tag otr: applied → end. Otherwise → Update Opportunity: stage Fan Only, status Abandoned.

C. GI OTR Applied V01.

Trigger: Pipeline Stage Changed, pipeline GI OTR Fans V01, stage Applied.

  1. Update Opportunity: status Won.
  2. Add Tag: otr: applied.
  3. Remove from Workflow: GI OTR Invite V01.

Later, once the .net sending domain is verified (don't build this yet):

  • In A, before the if/else: Wait until 10:00 AM the next day → Send Email with the apply link → Update Opportunity to stage Invited. That replaces step 4's manual text.
  • In B, swap the nudge task for a reminder email.
  • Use Google Forms' Get pre-filled link on Erik's form, so the email link fills in name, email and generation with {{contact.first_name}}, {{contact.email}} and {{contact.gi_generation}}. Test it with a name that has a space or apostrophe first.

Phase 2 (optional): a short Apps Script on Erik's response sheet posts each new response to a GHL Inbound Webhook trigger, which moves the card to Applied automatically. Until then, matching names by hand once a week is fine at this volume.

Step 5: Nightly log and scoreboard

GHL counts the scans for you. You only count the things it can't see: how many people stopped, how many answered on camera, usable clips, and how the owner reacted. Both go into the Measures table in the On the Road field plan, one row per night.

Contacts → Smart Lists. Save three:

  1. OTR — last night: tag source: on the road, Date Added = yesterday. This gives you the Signups column. Add a GI Generation column to read the Gen Z count off it.
  2. OTR — no src: tag otr: no src. Should stay empty. Anything here means a bad card or a forwarded link.
  3. OTR — fan list: tag gi: fan list. The list sponsors and episode announcements go to.

Scoreboard: Opportunities → GI OTR Fans V01, grouped by Source. That's scans and applicants per venue, which is the number you show a restaurant owner. Bring it to the Monday 7 AM call.

Test before printing

No card gets printed until scan 1 below passes on your phone. A venue tag that doesn't land is the one failure you can't fix after the night is over. Scan a QR on your phone over cellular, not desktop Wi-Fi, because that's how real people will do it.

  • Scan 1: QR for ?src=test-venue, Generation X. Check: road. redirected to the form, and the URL still ends in ?src=test-venue.
  • Contact created with first name, phone, email and GI Generation = Generation X.
  • GI Source Venue = test-venue on the contact. This is the critical check.
  • Tags: source: on the road and gi: fan list are there. otr: no src is not.
  • One card in GI OTR Fans V01 at Scanned, named after the contact, Source = test-venue.
  • The notification email arrived, with the generation and venue showing as text, not raw {{…}} tokens. Check spam before calling it missing.
  • The task "Text … the apply link" is on your task list.
  • Scan 2: open the bare road.generationallyincorrect.net with no ?src=. Check: the otr: no src tag lands.
  • Move the scan 1 card to Invited, then Applied. Check: status turns Won, otr: applied lands, and Enrollment History shows it left GI OTR Invite V01.
  • Enrollment History on all three workflows: every action Executed, none skipped.
  • Keep the test contact. Move its card to Fan Only as the known-good reference, the same as GI TestOne.
